01 · Why a trading companyA directory leaves both barriers standing

Two things stop a ten-person social enterprise from exporting: (1) MOQ and small-batch, many-SKU orders; (2) export documents, customs and logistics. A listing platform answers neither. Every benchmark that works — GoCoop, NOVICA, Faire — actually earns its money on execution. In Korea that function has a legal form: a trading company (무역상사).

사경기업의 두 장벽은 MOQ·소량 다품종 대응과 수출서류·통관·물류입니다. 목록만 제공하면 두 장벽은 그대로 남습니다.

04 · PlaybookWhat can be set up now, what opens with a track record

1. Institution — trade business code

Any individual or company with a business registration can apply to KITA (online or in person) and receives the trade business code (무역업 고유번호) on receipt, under Article 24 of the Foreign Trade Management Regulations. No separate licence or capital requirement; with the number you can file export declarations. KITA members receive it automatically. Documents: copy of business registration; representative's ID for in-person; power of attorney for an agent. Trade call centre 1566-5114.

2. Money — T/T in advance

First trades: 50% deposit, 50% before shipment. L/C and Net 60 only from year two, after a credit check (K-SURE credit enquiry). Write a shipment-hold clause for unpaid balances into the contract.

3. Category order

First: crafts, living, fabric, upcycled goods — import licensing is simple and inspection criteria can be documented. Second: food and cosmetics, which need FDA / CPNP and labelling work. The contracts signed at Gyeongbuk and Milan were in bags and fabric, which supports this order.

4. Expansion — public channels

Accumulate export records, then apply as a professional trading company (전문무역상사): at least USD 1m in export records for the previous year or the 3-year average, and 20%+ of exports must be products of other SMEs. Applications once a year (April–May), designation for 3 years, no fee. Export voucher service provider: suppliers then pay for our marketing and logistics with their own voucher. Municipal joint-export agency and a shared pavilion at fairs.

Cooperatives have a separate door

Under Article 7② of the Foreign Trade Management Regulations, a cooperative can apply with a recommendation from its supervising ministry. This matters for a social-economy cooperative structure: the recommendation route does not require the corporate export record that a company needs.

협동조합은 주무부처 장관 추천으로 전문무역상사 신청이 가능한 별도 트랙이 있습니다(대외무역관리규정 제7조②).

06 · Risk controlFour rules before the first container

  1. Buy only against a confirmed order — no speculative purchasing in year one.
  2. T/T 50/50 — no L/C, no Net 60 without verified buyer credit.
  3. Written inspection criteria per category agreed with the supplier before production, not after the claim.
  4. Return and claim clauses in every supplier contract, and a shipment-hold clause for unpaid balances.
